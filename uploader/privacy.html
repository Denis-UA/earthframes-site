Privacy Policy

Sabo YouTube Uploader is a private tool used only by its owner to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channels.

The app requests access to the owner's YouTube account solely to upload videos. It does not collect, store, sell or share personal data of any other users, because it has no other users.

OAuth tokens are stored only in the owner's private automation system and are used only to upload videos to the owner's channels. They are never shared with third parties.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
