Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

EarthFrames Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by its owner to upload videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Earth Frames.

Data the app accesses

The app requests permission to upload videos to the YouTube channel of the Google account that authorises it. It does not read, collect or store personal data of any other person.

How the data is used

The authorisation token is stored only on the owner's own server and is used solely to upload videos to the owner's channel. Data is not sold, shared with, or disclosed to third parties.

Google API Services

The use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
